Reasons to write, in rough order of how quickly we will reply:

A number here is wrong. This is the most useful email you can send. Include the page and what you think the right figure is. Corrections get made and the page notes that it changed.

You are a designer and we have linked to your work. If you would like a link changed, credited differently, or removed, say so and it will be done. If you would like your patterns considered for a future guide, that is also welcome — we link out, we never reproduce pattern text or photographs.

You have a question a page did not answer. Genuinely useful, because it usually means the page has a gap. Questions that come up more than once get added to the article.

Anything commercial. Some materials lists carry Amazon affiliate links, disclosed on each page and on the editorial policy page. There is no advertising or sponsorship on this site.

We are a small independent site, not a company with a support desk. Replies come from a person, and sometimes not the same day.
