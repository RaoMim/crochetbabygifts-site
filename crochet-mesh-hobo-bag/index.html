Crochet Mesh Hobo Bag: Free Pattern With Puff Stitch Columns

Crochet an oval base, work open mesh rounds of double crochet and chains with one puff stitch at each end of every round, then draw the mouth in with a band and work a strap up from each side to sew together at the top. The puffs stack into a raised column down each side of the bag. In 2 mm macrame cord on a 4 mm hook the bag comes out about 22.5 cm tall and 22 cm across the base, with a 45 cm handle.

The parts

Part Rounds or rows Stitches Size
Base rounds 1–7 ch 24, then 84 sts about 22 × 8.5 cm
Mesh body rounds 8–22 42 spaces a round, 2 of them puffs about 20.5 cm tall
Band rounds 23–25 84 to 72 sts about 1.5 cm tall
Straps rows 1–40, make 2 14 to 8 sts about 22.5 cm each

Each mesh round has two equal halves, so one puff lands over each end of the oval and the columns run straight up the sides.

What you need

Every tool our patterns use is on the Crochet essentials page.

  • 2 mm macrame cord, one colour, about the amount in the cord section. A cotton cord keeps the mesh square.
  • A 4 mm crochet hook. The hook size chart gives the US letter near it.
  • A tapestry needle with an eye big enough for the cord, scissors and two stitch markers.

Abbreviations

US crochet terms throughout. The UK name is in brackets.

  • ch: chain. sl st: slip stitch. st(s): stitch(es). inc: 2 sc in the same stitch. dec: sc 2 together.
  • sc: single crochet (UK: double crochet). dc: double crochet (UK: treble).
  • Puff: [yarn over, put the hook into the stitch, yarn over and pull up a loop to the height of a double crochet] 3 times, then yarn over and pull through all 7 loops on the hook.
  • Space: one stitch with the ch 1 after it. A mesh round counts in spaces.

The pattern

Work the base in continuous rounds with a marker in the first stitch. Join each mesh and band round with a sl st in the first stitch. Start each mesh round with ch 2 and each band round with ch 1; the starting chains do not count as stitches.

1. Base

  • Foundation: ch 24.
  • Rnd 1: sc in 2nd ch from hook, sc 21, 3 sc in last ch; working along the other side of the chain, sc 21, 2 sc in last ch. [48 sts]
  • Rnd 2: inc, sc 21, [inc] 3 times, sc 21, [inc] 2 times. [54 sts]
  • Rnd 3: sc 1, inc, sc 21, [sc 1, inc] 3 times, sc 21, [sc 1, inc] 2 times. [60 sts]
  • Rnd 4: sc 2, inc, sc 21, [sc 2, inc] 3 times, sc 21, [sc 2, inc] 2 times. [66 sts]
  • Rnd 5: sc 3, inc, sc 21, [sc 3, inc] 3 times, sc 21, [sc 3, inc] 2 times. [72 sts]
  • Rnd 6: sc 4, inc, sc 21, [sc 4, inc] 3 times, sc 21, [sc 4, inc] 2 times. [78 sts]
  • Rnd 7: sc 5, inc, sc 21, [sc 5, inc] 3 times, sc 21, [sc 5, inc] 2 times. [84 sts]

The round starts at one end of the oval. Lay the base flat: it should be about 22 cm long. If it cups, the increases are tight.

2. Mesh body

  • Rnd 8: [puff in next st, ch 1, skip 1, (dc in next st, ch 1, skip 1) 20 times] 2 times. [42 spaces]
  • Rnds 9–22: [puff in next puff, ch 1, (dc in next dc, ch 1) 20 times] 2 times. [42 spaces]

The first puff of each round sits over the start of the oval and the second over the far end, so the two puff columns face each other across the bag. Mark both puffs on round 8 and check they stay in line.

3. Band

  • Rnd 23: sc in each puff, each dc and each ch-1 space. [84 sts]
  • Rnd 24: [sc 5, dec] 12 times. [72 sts]
  • Rnd 25: sc in each st. [72 sts]

Do not fasten off. Find the sc above each puff column and put a marker in it.

4. Straps

Make one strap on each side, centred on the marker. For the first, sl st along to the 7th stitch before a marker.

  • Row 1: sc 14. [14 sts]
  • Row 2: ch 1, turn, dec, sc 10, dec. [12 sts]
  • Row 3: ch 1, turn, dec, sc 8, dec. [10 sts]
  • Row 4: ch 1, turn, dec, sc 6, dec. [8 sts]
  • Rows 5–40: ch 1, turn, sc 8. [8 sts]

Fasten off with a 40 cm tail. Join the cord 7 stitches before the other marker and make the second strap the same way. The 22 stitches between the straps on each side are the mouth of the bag.

Lay the bag flat, bring the two strap ends together without twisting them, and sew them end to end with the tail, going through both several times. Weave in every end.

Cord and time

It takes about 170 m of cord, about 140 to 190 g. The amount comes from counting every stitch and allowing a length of cord per stitch scaled from published DK figures, so it is an estimate, not weighed.

It is about 2,000 stitches, most of them in the mesh. Make the base and three mesh rounds, measure them, and adjust the hook before going on.

Frequently asked questions

Will things fall through the mesh? Keys and coins will. Sew a fabric lining inside, or keep small things in a pouch, such as our crochet drawstring pouch. For a bag in solid fabric, make the crochet ribbed shoulder bag in the same cord.

Can I make the handle longer? Yes. Add rows to rows 5–40 on both straps, the same number on each. Every 2 rows adds about 1 cm to each strap.

Can I add flowers? Yes. Sew small crochet flowers onto the band or the mesh. Our layered rose works on a bag.
