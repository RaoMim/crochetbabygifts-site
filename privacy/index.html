Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 October 2026.

The short version

This site is static HTML. It has no advertising and no login. It counts visits two ways: with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which sets no cookies, and with Google Analytics, which does set cookies. In the EU, the UK and Switzerland, Google Analytics cookies stay off unless you accept them. You can change your choice at any time: change your cookie choice.

What that means in detail

Cookies. The site itself sets none. Google Analytics sets two first-party cookies, _ga and _ga_E6156532FH, so it can tell a returning visitor from a new one. Google’s default lifetime for them is up to two years. If you are in the EU, the UK or Switzerland, they are only set after you press Accept on the bar at the foot of the page; until then Google Analytics receives visits without cookies. Elsewhere they are on by default, and you can turn them off. Your choice is kept in your own browser’s local storage, not in a cookie, and you can change your cookie choice here at any time. Google also offers a browser add-on that blocks Google Analytics on every site: search for “Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on”.

Analytics. Two tools.

  • Cloudflare Web Analytics, added 10 October 2026. It counts page views and records which page was visited, the referring site, the country, the browser and device type, and how fast the page loaded. Cloudflare states that it uses no cookies or local storage for this and does not fingerprint visitors by IP address or user agent.
  • Google Analytics 4, added 26 October 2026. It records the pages you view, the site that sent you, your approximate location (country and city), your device and browser, and some actions on the page: scrolling, clicking a link to another site, and downloading a PDF. Google states that Google Analytics 4 does not log or store IP addresses. We have turned off Google signals, ad personalisation and every optional data-sharing setting. Google keeps the detailed records for two months, its default. Google acts as our data processor under its data processing terms, and Google’s own privacy policy applies to how it runs the service.

We look at totals and trends, not at individuals, and we do not combine this data with anything else.

Advertising. None at present. If advertising is added in future, this page will be updated before any ad code goes live, and it will name the ad provider and say what data that provider collects. Advertising networks generally do set cookies and process data for personalised ads; when that applies here, it will be described here.

Third-party requests. The pages load one font file from this same domain. The requests to other servers are the analytics scripts: Cloudflare’s (static.cloudflareinsights.com) and Google’s (www.googletagmanager.com, reporting to google-analytics.com), which report the visit as described above. No font CDN, no ad server, no image host. One tutorial page shows a YouTube video, and it loads nothing from YouTube until you press its play button. Once you press play, the video comes from YouTube’s privacy-enhanced player (youtube-nocookie.com) and YouTube’s own privacy policy applies to that video.

Server logs. The site is served by Cloudflare, which, like all hosting providers, records ordinary technical request data such as IP address, user agent and the URL requested. This is standard operational logging, not something this site reads or analyses.

Email. If you email us, we have your email and whatever you wrote, for as long as needed to reply and keep a record of corrections. It is not added to any list, because there is no list.

Children. This site is written for adults buying or making gifts for babies. It is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone.

Your rights

We hold no names or contact details for visitors. Google Analytics data is tied to a random cookie ID, not to you by name, so we cannot look you up in it; you can stop it being collected with the cookie choice above or Google’s add-on. If you have emailed us and want that email deleted, ask and it will be.

Changes

Material changes — in practice, that means adding advertising or any new tracking — will be reflected here with a new date at the top, before the change goes live.
